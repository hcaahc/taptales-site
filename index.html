The studio

Small team, big on play.

Taptales started with a simple belief: the best digital products feel like a good story. They're easy to start, fun to explore and hard to put down. We're a compact team of designers, developers and artists, made in Vietnam and played worldwide.

Fun is a feature

Even a utility app deserves a moment of joy. We design for that on purpose.

Players first

We read every review and ship updates based on what players actually ask for.

Stories that travel

Our characters live across games, apps and videos, so fans can follow them everywhere.

Safe for families

Kids' titles are made with age-appropriate content and parent-friendly settings.
